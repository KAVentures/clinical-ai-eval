# Prospective review protocol and local provenance

For citation, scribe and paired robustness studies, an institutional administrator
can now freeze the research question, descriptive analysis intent and exact
independent reviewer roster from the research studio before any recorded
candidate exchange or collection. Robustness has separate reference-validity
and isolated-answer reviewer rosters. The existing scientific roster validators
and active account checks apply; the study owner cannot be a reviewer.

The freeze is optional for backward compatibility. The status distinguishes
`available`, `locally_prospective`, `not_preregistered` and
`not_applicable` (native deterministic decision studies). The administrator
must externally confirm independence, reference provenance and no known prior
candidate exposure. The registry binds the exact scientific plan hash, study
identity, role roster, research question and analysis intent to an immutable
digest and a local audit sequence. A second registration is refused. Candidate
input/template exports and supervised queueing or manual import preclude a later
prospective registration even if the candidate was not actually called.

After collection the existing review assignment action must use the same
reviewer IDs and roles. No silent substitution is possible. If the roster
can no longer serve, keep the original record and create a separate study;
do not edit the database or recycle the old claim. The standard retrospective
review route remains available for studies without registration.

The administrator-only review provenance download verifies the local ledger,
its audit ordering, canonical issued and submitted review integrity where
applicable, and the verified scientific report digest when analyzed. The
receipt is distinct from the canonical report and does not change any scorer,
family maturity, comparison or procurement gate. No reviewer receives another
reviewer's material through this feature. Store exports through approved
institutional channels; the administrative receipt contains account IDs and
free-text study intent.

## Important limits

Local audit order is not an external timestamp, a public preregistration or proof
that a clinician never saw an answer elsewhere. External review credentials,
conflict checks, case provenance and genuine blinding remain institutional
responsibilities. The frozen text states descriptive research intent; it is not
a machine-enforced inferential analysis plan. The scientific protocols and
their fixed denominators remain authoritative. Trusted filesystem/database
administration is assumed. This feature is experimental and does not establish
clinical validity or deployment readiness.
